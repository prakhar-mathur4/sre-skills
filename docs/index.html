How it behaves

Safe to have near production.

The skill gives the agent the habits good SREs follow on every page, every change and every postmortem.

Mitigate first

Roll back, drain traffic or flip a flag before hunting for the root cause.

SRE book Ch 14

Know the blast radius

Every command comes with what it affects, whether it can be undone, and how. Risky ones are marked ⚠️.

Workbook Ch 9

Change safely

Gradual, observable, reversible. Binary and config pushes cause about 68% of outages.

Workbook App C

Confirm before acting

Read-only checks first. Anything that writes to production waits for your yes.

Workbook Ch 9

Verify, don't assume

Checks that the user-facing signal recovered, and keeps what it saw apart from what it suspects.

SRE book Ch 12

Blameless, always

Asks what the system allowed, not who did it. Action items get owners and types.

SRE book Ch 15
What it does

From the first page to the postmortem.

You don't have to say "SRE". The skill kicks in for any reliability or production-operations question.

Live incidents
Latency, 5xx, CPU, disk, DNS, TCP, MySQL replication lag, Kafka backlogs. Mitigation first, then read-only triage.
SLOs and budgets
SLIs based on what users feel, realistic targets, and a written error budget policy. Pushes back on five nines with numbers.
Alerting
Multiwindow burn-rate pages instead of CPU alerts, ticket tiers, and options for low-traffic services. Prometheus rules included.
Gap reviews
What's missing to make this reliable? Every component checked for SLOs, monitoring, redundancy, deploy safety and backups.
Postmortems
Blameless write-ups from messy notes: UTC timeline, trigger separated from causes, owned and typed action items.
Rollouts
Canary plans with a concurrent control group, abort criteria and rollback. Config gets the same care as code.
Capacity
Machines, disk and network estimated with real numbers, N+2 headroom, and what happens when each failure domain dies.
Teams
On-call sizing, toil budgets, pager load reviews, and how SRE works with dev teams.
Real numbers

Math, not vibes.
