# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else this project is represented) is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- In the spirit of blameless postmortems: focus on what the system allowed, not on who made a mistake.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language, or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately via the contact details on the [prakhar-mathur4 GitHub profile](https://github.com/prakhar-mathur4). Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
